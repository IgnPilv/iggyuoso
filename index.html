Learn piano in your browser with a MIDI keyboard

iggyuoso is a free piano trainer for adult beginners, including people who think they are "not musical". Plug a MIDI keyboard into your computer, open the site in your browser and start playing: there is nothing to install and no account is needed.

It is a trainer, not a song player. Short exercises each train one skill, both hands work together early, and each hand is scored on its own. The feedback says which hand or which habit gave way: early or late notes, rushing, uneven playing, wrong notes, short holds, the sustain pedal and loud and soft playing.

What you learn

  • Playing: technique, scales, chords and pieces, from the first key to an intermediate level.
  • Reading music: notes on the treble and bass staff, sight-reading drills and key signatures.
  • Rhythm: tapping, counting, upbeats, syncopation, compound time and swing.
  • Music theory: a 65-chapter textbook read alongside the lessons, in the order of music-school piano books.
  • Ear training: from "same or different?" up to intervals, chords and cadences, answered on the keys.
  • Making music: improvising that is judged by musical rules, composing, and a sketchbook for your own ideas.

How it works

  • Falling notes or sheet music, with finger numbers, chord names and both hands drawn on the keys.
  • A growing mastery tree with seven branches (technique, reading, rhythm, theory, ear, pieces, creativity) instead of a fixed course.
  • Today's practice plans about 20 minutes a day, with reviews of skills that are fading.
  • Songs taught in steps, your own MIDI files, and a practice room with games and tools.
  • Works with any MIDI keyboard; a short calibration finds its range and middle C. A computer-keyboard mode lets you try it without a piano.
  • Progress stays on your device, or a free account keeps it in step across devices.

MIDI keyboards work in Chrome, Edge, Opera and Firefox on a computer, and in Chrome on Android. Watch the demo to see every feature played on a virtual keyboard. Easy as C D E.
